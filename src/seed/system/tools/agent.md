Make an agent: a row on the roster, a folder of its own, and a mind that starts at once. It
takes the org's settings, and whoever owns its accounts steers it. To give a member an agent of
their own, make it with their email or phone, then `connect` for it: the sign-in lands only as
the account that handle names. An agent that exists is changed at the terminal, never here.

- id: its name, which is also its folder and its user: lowercase letters, digits and dashes,
  starting with a letter
- name: the name it goes by
- email: its member's email, the handle a sign-in for it must match
- phone: its member's phone, the number a WhatsApp pairing for it is minted for
