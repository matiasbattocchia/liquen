Unset something that is still standing: a pending approval of yours (a call awaiting a
verdict that stopped being worth asking; the card closes and the call never runs), or a
message that has not gone out yet, in a room you can write to, whoever sent it (it is
withdrawn and never goes out, and the session that sent it is told).

- id: the id, exactly as your pending list, or your window, shows it
