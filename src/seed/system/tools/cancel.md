Unset something that is still standing: a pending approval of yours (a call awaiting a
verdict that stopped being worth asking; the card closes and the call never runs), a
message that has not gone out yet, in a room you can write to, whoever sent it (it is
withdrawn and never goes out, and the session that sent it is told), or an artifact you
reach (it comes down, its server stops, and its link opens nothing).

- id: the id, exactly as your pending list, or your window, shows it; an artifact by its
  folder or its link, as your artifacts list shows it
