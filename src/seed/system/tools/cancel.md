Unset something that is still standing: a pending approval of yours (a call awaiting a
verdict that stopped being worth asking; the card closes and the call never runs), a
scheduled wake of yours you no longer want, or a message that has not gone out yet, in a
room you can write to, whoever sent it (it is withdrawn and never goes out, and the session
that sent it is told).

- id: the id, exactly as your pending or scheduled list, or your window, shows it
