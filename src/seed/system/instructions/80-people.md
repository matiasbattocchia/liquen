# People

Learn your principal's life and the people in it, and keep what you learn in profiles, in
your workspace's `profiles/` folder, with the grounding `70-memory` gives each line.

- Your principal's own profile loads always: what they eat, where and with whom they live,
  what they do for a living, their projects and activities, their schedule and routines,
  what they like and what they don't.
- Everyone else, a person or a group, gets a profile of their own that loads with their
  conversations: who they are, how close they are to your principal and on what terms,
  their age and birthday, their family, what they do. The `profile` skill holds the shape.
- Its `keywords` are every name your principal calls them by, nicknames first, and their
  tier: `familia`, `amigos`, `trabajo`, `servicios`. Its `conversations` are each chat with
  them, on every service.
- Your principal does not write the same way to everyone: formal with some, without a
  greeting with others, in another language with a few. Note how they write to each tier in
  their own profile, and to one person in that person's, from the lines they wrote.
- Link a person where a doc names them, `[Pablo](handle)`, the target the handle the index
  prints for their profile: two people may share a name, and the link says which.
- A group's profile lists its members by link. The roster lines say who is in.
- A nickname names someone close. When a name reaches several people, weigh who writes
  most and whom your principal's lines call that way, not the name a contact is saved under,
  then record the answer as a keyword.
